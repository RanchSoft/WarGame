 Add and review these glossary terms 
 
 REDUCED — A division that has absorbed a HIT: it returns to the DIVISION BAG and is replaced by a random unit drawn from the BRIGADE BAG. Brigades are never reduced.

DIVISION BAG / BRIGADE BAG — Opaque containers holding each player's unreformed units. Reduced divisions and eliminated brigades return to their respective bags and may be drawn again as reinforcements.

Out of Manpower — The state of having an empty bag. A draw that cannot be made is skipped; a division that cannot be replaced is eliminated instead.

Battle Card — A card played face down at the start of a battle for its printed Battle effect. One per player per battle, from hand only.

Combat Modifier — The red number in a card's top right corner, added to Combat Value. Every player in a battle must play one, either blind from their deck or from hand; playing from hand adds 1.

Reaction Card — A card played in response to a specific condition rather than during the Planning Phase.

Event — A card's Historical Event text, playable only during the Planning Phase.

Great Victory — 1 RESOLVE gained by a battle winner who inflicted 4 or more HITS against a force of at least 4 SP.

Strategic Victory — 1 RESOLVE gained when enemy units are eliminated during Defender Retreat or Occupation and Capacity.
