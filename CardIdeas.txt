Generic Cards:

Types of Cards: BATTLE, EVENT, 

Name: Desertions
Type: Reaction
Orders Number: 3 / Reinforcement Value:  D D / Combat Value: 2 
Effect: Play immediately after winning a battle but before casualties are assigned, your opponent must suffer an additional 2 SP of casualties.


Name: Leaked Plans
Type: EVENT
Orders Number: 3 / Reinforcement Value:  B B / Combat Value: 1
Effect: For the rest of the turn your opponent places their order markers face up.


Name: Creeping Barrage
Type: Battle
Orders Number: 4 / Reinforcement Value: D D D  / Combat Value: 2
Effect: Play only in a battle where you are the attacker. FORTIFICATION TOKENS and DEFEND AREA ORDERS do not prevent SP loss this combat.

Name: Ceaseless Rain
Type: EVENT
Orders Number:  2 / Reinforcement Value: D B  / Combat Value: 0
Effect: When conducting movement this turn, all areas cost a minimum of 2 movement to enter.


Name: Dig In
Type: EVENT
Orders Number: 4  / Reinforcement Value: D D  / Combat Value:  3
Effect: Immediately place 3 fortification tokens on 3 different friendly areas (this may not exceed the maximum fortification level).

Name. Preliminary Bombardment
Type: EVENT
Orders Number: 3  / Reinforcement Value: D B B  / Combat Value:  1
Effect: Immediately remove all fortification counters from a area that neighbors friendly units.

Name: Communication Breakdown
Type: Battle
Orders Number: 2  / Reinforcement Value: B B   / Combat Value:  0
Effect: Play only in a battle where you are defending. Ignore 1 SP of losses for every direction you are being attacked from.
